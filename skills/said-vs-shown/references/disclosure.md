# Disclosure and publishing checklist

This skill never posts. Use this checklist when the user publishes. Platform policies were checked as of October 2026; re-check them before relying on this.

## In every video and page

- **Closing card and companion page:** carry `episode.json` → `disclosure`, e.g. "AI-produced · AI voice · every claim sourced · reviewed by <name>". Keep "reviewed by" only if a human actually reviewed the episode (stage 10).
- **Description or caption:** the same line, plus the source list or a link to the companion page.

## Per platform

| Platform | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Tick "Altered or synthetic content" when uploading, and put the disclosure line in the description. Make every episode's analysis distinct: the Partner Program excludes "mass-produced" templated AI content and content that only reads other material. | The scope for a non-impersonating synthetic narrator is ambiguous, so disclose (support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491; /1311392). |
| Instagram / Facebook | Turn on Meta's AI label. | Meta's own example of content needing the label is "a reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover". |
| TikTok | Generic TTS narration is exempt from the AIGC label; label it anyway. Never make it look like it comes from a real news outlet. | TikTok integrity and authenticity guidelines |
| LinkedIn / X | Not required; add the line anyway. | Their policies target depictions of real people or deceptive media. |
| EU audiences | AI-generated text that informs the public on matters of public interest must be disclosed, unless a human reviewed it and a named person holds editorial responsibility. | EU AI Act, Art. 50 (applies from Aug 2, 2026) |

## Screenshots and quotes

- Crop tight on the quoted sentence; avoid photos and ads inside the capture.
- Comment on every quote.
- Show the outlet or site and the date on screen, and link sources in the description.
- Never put an outlet's photos or logos in thumbnails.
- Follow the Code of Best Practices in Fair Use for Online Video (cmsimpact.org).
- Credit alone is not fair use.

## Sensitive editions

Finance and politics editions (e.g. markets): YouTube's "AI personas related to sensitive topics" rule makes monetization high-risk. Publish them, but keep them out of the Partner Program.
