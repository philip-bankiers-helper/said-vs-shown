# Said vs. Shown episode: HyperFrames project

This folder is one episode's video project, built by the `said-vs-shown` skill. Work through that skill (`/said-vs-shown`, `SKILL.md`), not by hand.

- Design truth: `frame.md`. Data contract: the skill's `references/project-layout.md`.
- Facts on screen come from `../edit/episode.json` and `../ledger/`; every claim traces to `../ledger/claims.json`.
- `index.html` is generated by the skill's `engine/assemble.py`. Never hand-edit it.
- Scenes live in `compositions/NN-<scene>.html`. The squeeze, payoff, one-thing and close are filled by `engine/fill_scenes.py`; boards and boundaries come from `board.py` / `boundary.py`; receipt scenes are authored from `templates/scenes/receipt.html`.
- HyperFrames is pinned (see `package.json`). For the composition contract, read the `hyperframes-core` skill.
